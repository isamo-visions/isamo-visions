# Samer Abdulridha · iSamo

**Senior AI Digital Creator · AI Visual Storytelling · Creative Direction**

I'm Samer Abdulridha, known as **Samo**, based in Baghdad, Iraq. **iSamo** is my creative brand and official portfolio.

I bring AI image and video creation together with more than 20 years of experience in cinema, lighting, broadcast production and photography. My work moves from the first visual idea to finished imagery, campaigns and interactive experiences.

## What I create

- AI-generated imagery and video, with a focus on visual storytelling.
- Creative concepts, visual identities and advertising content.
- Motion graphics and interactive visual experiences.
- Cinematography, lighting design and photography.

## Background

Since 2022, I have worked as a **Senior AI Digital Creator for the American University of Iraq – Baghdad (AUIB) through Iraqi Land Company**. My earlier work includes cinematography for BBC Arabic and Iraqi television channels, alongside studio leadership and broadcast production.

I hold a Bachelor of Cinema Arts from the College of Fine Arts, University of Baghdad, and a Diploma in Cinema Arts from the Institute of Fine Arts, Baghdad.
